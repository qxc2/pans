I am currently a Staff Scientist at Ames National Laboratory. My research focuses on developing sustainable separation technologies for critical materials and environmental applications. By combining molecular ligand design, interfacial chemistry, porous materials, and advanced characterization, my work aims to recover and separate rare earth elements and other critical metals from complex resources such as mining streams, solvent-extraction wastewater, and electronic waste. A central goal of my research is to connect fundamental understanding of metal–ligand interactions with practical separation processes that reduce chemical consumption, minimize secondary waste, and enable more circular and environmentally responsible critical-material supply chains.
